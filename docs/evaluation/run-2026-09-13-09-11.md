## New menu feedback (Restaurant owner)

Goal: We changed our menu. I want input on the new dishes, the choices available, and the prices. Find out what customers like and what we should improve.

Scenario time: 913 s.

### Drafted questions

1. Since the new menu launched last month, have you eaten at our restaurant and ordered from that menu? _(choice: Yes / No / Not sure)_
2. Which of the new dishes did you try? _(open)_
3. What did you like or not like about the new dishes you tried? _(open)_
4. How satisfied were you with the range of choices on the new menu, on a scale from 1 to 5? _(rating)_
5. What do you think about the vegetarian choices on the menu now? _(open)_
6. How did the portion sizes feel to you? _(choice: Too small / About right / Too large / It depended on the dish / Not sure)_
7. How did the prices feel for what you received? _(open)_
8. Is there anything else you would like to add, or any dishes from the old menu that you miss? _(open, optional)_

Judge: relevance 5/5, not leading 5/5, screening 5/5, spoken 5/5, length 5/5.

Issues:
- Q2 asks which new dishes they tried, but some respondents may have ordered from the new menu without trying one of the six new mains; they may have tried revised existing dishes or only noticed prices/choice changes.
- Q4's 1-to-5 satisfaction scale would be clearer on the phone if the endpoints are read aloud, for example '1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied.'
- Q5 is relevant, but for non-vegetarian customers it may produce weak answers unless it allows 'I did not look at vegetarian choices' or probes whether vegetarian choice matters to their group.
- Q7 is broad and good, but if price sensitivity is a key issue after a 10% increase, it may help to include a follow-up or scale about value for money.

Suggested improvements:
- Revise Q2 to: 'Which dishes did you order from the new menu, including any new dishes, if you remember?'
- Revise Q4 to: 'How satisfied were you with the range of choices on the new menu, where 1 means very dissatisfied and 5 means very satisfied?'
- Revise Q5 to: 'Did you notice the vegetarian choices on the menu? If yes, what did you think about them?'
- Consider adding: 'Compared with your previous visits, did the menu prices feel lower, about the same, a little higher, or much higher?'
- Consider adding a short prioritization question: 'If we changed one thing about the new menu, what should it be?'
