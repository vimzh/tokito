## New menu feedback (Restaurant owner)

Goal: We changed our menu. I want input on the new dishes, the choices available, and the prices. Find out what customers like and what we should improve.

Scenario time: 571 s.

### Drafted questions

1. Since last month, have you ordered from or eaten from our new menu? _(choice: Yes / No / Not sure)_
2. Which of the new main dishes did you try, and what did you think of them? _(open)_
3. What, if anything, did you especially like about the new menu? _(open)_
4. What, if anything, would you improve about the new menu? _(open)_
5. How did the portion sizes feel to you? _(choice: Too small / About right / Too large / It depended on the dish / Not sure)_
6. What do you think about the vegetarian choices on the new menu? _(open)_
7. On a scale of 1 to 5, how fair do the new prices feel for the food and portions you received? _(rating)_
8. Are there any dishes from the old menu that you miss, or anything else you would like us to know? _(open, optional)_

Judge: relevance 5/5, not leading 5/5, screening 5/5, spoken 5/5, length 5/5.

Issues:
- Q2 assumes the respondent tried one or more new main dishes; respondents who used the new menu but only ordered older items, sides, drinks, or desserts may not know how to answer.
- Q6 is relevant but broad; it may not distinguish between vegetarian customers, non-vegetarian customers noticing variety, and people who did not look at vegetarian choices.
- Q7 asks about fairness of prices but only from those who received food and portions; it may be less answerable for someone who only browsed the menu or does not remember prices.

Suggested improvements:
- Add interviewer guidance after Q1: if No or Not sure, ask whether they have looked at the new menu, then skip dish/portion/price experience questions as appropriate.
- Revise Q2 to: “Which dishes from the new menu, if any, did you try? What did you think of them?”
- Consider adding a closed follow-up on choice variety: “Did the menu have enough choices for you and your group? Yes / No / Not sure. What was missing?”
- For Q6, ask: “Did you or anyone in your group look for vegetarian options? If yes, what did you think of the vegetarian choices?”
- For Q7, allow “Not sure / did not notice prices” and consider a follow-up: “What makes you say that?”
