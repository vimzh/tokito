Issues:
- Q1 screens appropriately, but the questionnaire does not specify whether respondents who answer No/Not sure should skip dish-specific questions or answer only general/menu-awareness questions.
- Q6 asks all respondents about vegetarian choices; non-vegetarian customers or those who did not notice the vegetarian section may need an explicit 'not sure/not applicable' option.
- Q7 measures overall price fairness well, but it may not distinguish whether prices felt high because of taste, portion size, comparison to old menu, or delivery markups.

Suggested improvements:
- Add interviewer routing after Q1: if Yes, continue; if No/Not sure, skip to a brief awareness/general preferences question or end politely.
- For Q6, add probes/options such as: 'If you looked at them, how do you feel about the vegetarian choices—too few, about right, or enough variety? If you did not notice, that is okay.'
- Consider adding one short overall satisfaction or revisit-intent question, such as: 'Based on the new menu, how likely are you to order from us again?'
- For prices, consider a follow-up open probe: 'What made the prices feel fair or unfair?'
